## Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, students will be able to:

- Conduct a systematic and reproducible literature review
- Critically analyze state-of-the-art research (50 papers)

---

## Assignment Description

Students are required to conduct a systematic literature review using **ResearchRabbit** and analyze a minimum of **50 high-quality research papers**.

The assignment goes beyond summary — it requires:

- Deep critical analysis
- Comparative evaluation
- Synthesis of insights
- Preparation of a publishable paper

---

## Task Requirements

### 1. Literature Collection (10%)

Use ResearchRabbit to:

- Identify relevant papers
- Explore citation networks
- Track seminal and recent works (last 5 years preferred)

Include:

- Minimum **50 papers**
- At least **60% recent** (last 5 years)

### 2. Systematic Review Methodology (15%)

Students must clearly define:

- Search strategy (keywords, filters)
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Screening process (PRISMA-style preferred)
- Categorization approach

### 3. Comparative Analysis Table (20%)

Construct a comprehensive comparison table with the following columns:

| Year | Paper | Contribution | Dataset | Data Size | Model(s) Used | Evaluation Metrics | Key Results |
|------|-------|--------------|---------|-----------|---------------|--------------------|-------------|

**Important requirements:**

- Minimum **50 entries**
- Must include:
  - Dataset characteristics (size, domain)
  - Model types (e.g., Transformer, CNN, LLM)
  - Evaluation metrics (Accuracy, F1, WER, etc.)
- Table must **support analysis**, not just listing

### 4. Critical Analysis & Synthesis (25%)

Compare approaches across:

- Models
- Datasets
- Evaluation methods

Identify:

- Strengths and weaknesses
- Trends (e.g., shift to LLMs, multimodal systems)
- Limitations in current research

Provide **deep insights**, not descriptive summaries.

### 5. Research Gap & Future Work (10%)

- Identify clear research gaps
- Propose:
  - New frameworks / improvements
  - Future research directions
- Should demonstrate original thinking

### 6. Publication-Ready Paper (20%)

Submit a **5,000-word** research paper with the following structure:

1. Abstract
2. Introduction
3. Research Objectives
4. Literature Review
5. Methodology (Systematic Review Process)
6. Comparative Analysis
7. Discussion
8. Research Gaps & Future Work
9. Conclusion
10. References (IEEE / APA)
